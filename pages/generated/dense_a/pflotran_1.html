Case A: pflotran result 1

For more information about pflotran, see the pflotran group page.

groupname = "pflotran" # hide
resultid = 1 # hide
using CSP11Visualizer, GLMakie, CairoMakie # hide
CairoMakie.activate!() # hide
steps = [5, 120] # hide
results = CSP11Visualizer.parse_dense_timesteps(groupname, resultid, "a", steps = steps, verbose = false); # hide
end_of_injection = findfirst(isequal(5), steps) # hide
@assert !isnothing(end_of_injection) # hide
end_of_migration = findfirst(isequal(120), steps) # hide
@assert !isnothing(end_of_migration) # hide

Overview animation

The animation below shows the migration of CO₂ in the domain over time, plotted as the mass fraction of CO₂ in the liquid phase. The animation starts at the initial state and ends at the end of the migration period. We also plot the amount of dissolved CO₂ in the liquid phase in the two reporting boxes A and B, as well as the mobile CO₂ in the gas phase. Note that the playback speed is slower during injection than during migration.
